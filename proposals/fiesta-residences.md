# Business ROI Report: Fiesta Residences Boutique Hotel & Serviced Apartments

**Audit Date:** 2026-03-26
**Sector:** Hospitality — Boutique Hotel & Serviced Apartments
**Location:** Fifth Avenue, West Cantonments, Accra (near East Legon)

---

## Business Summary

Fiesta Residences offers 24 hotel rooms and 30 serviced two-bedroom apartments (54 units total). Rated 4.3/5 (Excellent) on MakeMyTrip, location rated 9.3/10. Has an official website (fiestaresidences.com), listed on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Expedia, Hotels.com. Offers spa, outdoor pool, restaurant, airport shuttle, and 24-hour front desk. Rates from ~$180/night. TripAdvisor reviewers say it "should not get less than 5 stars."

**Digital Maturity Level:** Moderately Digital — Has a functioning website, OTA presence, and 24-hour front desk. Airport shuttle coordination requires pre-arrival contact. Reviews are overwhelmingly positive with few complaints about digital gaps.

| Gap | Description | Severity |

## ROI Estimates

### FAQ Automation
- **Estimated inquiry volume:** ~15-20 inquiries/day (54 units, mix of short and long stay)
- **Average handling time:** ~4 min per inquiry
- **Monthly staff hours on repetitive inquiries:** ~30-40 hours/month
- **AI chatbot could handle ~60%:** Saves **~18-24 hours/month**

### 24/7 AI Voice/Chat Agent
- **After-hours missed inquiries:** Already has 24-hour front desk, so gap is smaller
- **Potential recovery:** ~**$200-400/month** from faster response times and automated airport shuttle booking

---

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. Automated Airport Shuttle Booking
- **What:** When a booking is confirmed, auto-send a WhatsApp/email form to capture flight details and schedule the shuttle — no manual back-and-forth needed
- **Benefit:** Eliminates pre-arrival coordination friction; saves 5-10 hrs/month
- **Quick win** — 1 week

### 2. Long-Stay Apartment Management Automation
- **What:** For the 30 serviced apartments, automate recurring housekeeping schedules, maintenance requests, and lease renewal reminders via a Supabase-backed system
- **Benefit:** Reduces coordination overhead as apartment occupancy scales
- **Long-term project** — 3-4 weeks

### 3. Automated Post-Stay Review Requests
- **What:** Send a personalized thank-you + review link via WhatsApp/email 24 hours after checkout
- **Benefit:** Increases review volume (currently strong ratings but could have more reviews); boosts SEO
- **Quick win** — 1 week

---

## Manual Noise Score: 4/10

Fiesta Residences is the most digitally mature property in this audit. Their 24-hour front desk, professional website, and strong OTA presence mean fewer gaps. The main opportunities are in automating the airport shuttle workflow and optimizing long-stay apartment management. This is a "good to great" candidate, not a turnaround.

---

## Next Steps

1. Automate the airport shuttle booking with a simple post-confirmation form
2. Build a recurring housekeeping/maintenance schedule for the apartment units
3. Set up an automated post-checkout review request via WhatsApp
