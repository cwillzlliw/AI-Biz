# Business ROI Report: HGL Guest House

**Audit Date:** 2026-03-26
**Sector:** Hospitality — Guest House
**Location:** Nii Afutu Tsuru Street, East Legon, Accra

---

## Business Summary

HGL Guest House is a recently renovated 3-star property in East Legon offering queen/king rooms and studio/two-bedroom apartments for short and extended stays. Rated 7.1/10 (Good) on Booking.com with 25+ reviews. Amenities include restaurant, bar, garden, terrace, free WiFi, airport shuttle, 24-hour front desk, minimarket, and car rental. 15 min from Kotoka Airport, 10 min from Accra Mall. No own website found — bookings primarily through Booking.com and TripAdvisor.

**Digital Maturity Level:** Minimal-to-Partial Digital — No own website. Relies on OTAs for bookings. Has a 24-hour front desk and airport shuttle, but guest reviews reveal operational gaps (soundproofing, room prep, WiFi issues, staff disorganization).

---

## Automation Gaps Found

| Gap | Description | Severity |

---

## ROI Estimates

### FAQ Automation
- **Estimated inquiry volume:** ~8-12 inquiries/day (mix of short and extended stay guests)
- **Average handling time:** ~5 min per inquiry
- **Monthly staff hours on repetitive inquiries:** ~20-30 hours/month
- **AI chatbot could handle ~70%:** Saves **~14-21 hours/month**

### 24/7 AI Voice/Chat Agent
- **After-hours missed inquiries:** Extended-stay guests often need support outside business hours (maintenance, WiFi help)
- **Estimated recovery/value:** **$150-300/month** from faster inquiry response and reduced OTA dependency

### Operational Efficiency
- **Housekeeping automation** could eliminate room-not-ready complaints, improving review scores from 7.1 toward 8.0+
- **Higher ratings = higher occupancy** — estimated **$300-500/month** in additional bookings

---

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. WhatsApp Business + AI Inquiry Bot
- **What:** Set up WhatsApp Business for guest inquiries, room availability, rates, and directions. Add auto-replies for common questions.
- **Benefit:** 14-21 hrs/month saved; 24/7 reachability; reduces OTA dependency
- **Quick win** — 1 week

### 2. Digital Housekeeping Checklist & Room Readiness System
- **What:** Simple Supabase-backed system where housekeeping marks rooms as cleaned/ready. Front desk can see real-time status before assigning rooms.
- **Benefit:** Eliminates "room not prepared" complaints; improves reviews
- **Quick win** — 1-2 weeks

### 3. Google Business Profile + One-Page Website
- **What:** Optimize Google Business listing. Create a simple site with rooms, rates, amenities, and a WhatsApp booking link to reduce OTA commissions.
- **Benefit:** Direct bookings; better discoverability; reduced 15-20% OTA fees
- **Quick win** — 1 week

---

## Manual Noise Score: 8/10

HGL Guest House has a recently renovated physical property but its operations are still largely manual. The lack of a website, staff disorganization complaints, room readiness failures, and WiFi/maintenance issues all point to workflows that depend entirely on human coordination. The extended-stay apartment model especially needs better systems — long-term guests expect consistent, reliable service.

---

## Next Steps

1. Set up WhatsApp Business with auto-replies for rates, availability, and directions
2. Implement a simple room-readiness tracking system (even a shared spreadsheet is a start)
3. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile with updated photos and amenities
