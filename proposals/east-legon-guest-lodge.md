# Business ROI Report: East Legon Guest Lodge

**Audit Date:** 2026-03-26
**Sector:** Hospitality — Guest House
**Location:** Lagos Avenue, Shiashie – East Legon, Accra

---

## Business Summary

East Legon Guest Lodge is a small 7-room guest house known primarily for its restaurant. Rated 5.2/10 on HotelsCombined. Described in reviews as "basic and slightly outdated" but with "very friendly staff and owner/manager." Rooms at ~$34-80/night. No own website found — bookings go through OTAs (Expedia, Hotels.com, Traveloka, Hostelworld, KAYAK). Payment is cash-only upon arrival. Continental breakfast included. Offers free airport pickup.

**Digital Maturity Level:** Minimal Digital — No own website, no direct booking engine, no visible WhatsApp Business presence. Relies entirely on OTAs and phone (+233 302 505 350). Cash-only payment. This is a classic paper-and-phone operation.

---

## Automation Gaps Found

| Gap | Description | Severity |
|-----|-------------|----------|
| No own website | Zero direct web presence; 100% OTA dependent (15-20% commission per booking) | High |
| Cash-only payment | No digital payment; guests must pay in cash on arrival | High |
| No WhatsApp Business | No visible WhatsApp contact for inquiries or booking | Medium |
| No booking engine | Cannot accept direct reservations; all bookings via OTAs or phone | High |
| Restaurant order management | "Best known for the restaurant" but no online menu, ordering, or reservation system | Medium |
| No guest communication system | No pre-arrival info, no follow-up, no review solicitation | Medium |
| Outdated property perception | Reviews mention "outdated" — no digital tools to offset this impression | Low |

---

## ROI Estimates

### FAQ Automation
- **Estimated inquiry volume:** ~5-8 inquiries/day (small 7-room property)
- **Average handling time:** ~5 min per inquiry (phone-based, slower)
- **Monthly staff hours on repetitive inquiries:** ~12-20 hours/month
- **AI chatbot could handle ~75%:** Saves **~9-15 hours/month** of owner/staff time

### 24/7 AI Voice/Chat Agent
- **After-hours missed inquiries:** At 7 rooms and $50-80/night average, even 2 missed bookings/month = **$100-160/month in lost revenue**
- **OTA commission savings:** If a direct booking system captured just 3 bookings/month away from OTAs (saving 15% commission) = **$22-36/month saved**
- **Combined recovery:** **~$130-200/month**

### Restaurant Revenue
- **Restaurant is the star asset** — if they captured online orders or reservations, could increase covers by 10-15%
- **Estimated additional revenue:** **$200-400/month**

---

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. WhatsApp Business + Simple Booking Bot
- **What:** Create a WhatsApp Business account that auto-responds to inquiries with room types, rates, availability, and directions. Accept booking requests via chat.
- **Benefit:** 9-15 hrs/month saved; 24/7 inquiry handling; reduces OTA dependency
- **Quick win** — can ship in 1 week

### 2. Mobile Money Payment Integration
- **What:** Accept MTN Mobile Money and Vodafone Cash for reservations and restaurant orders — eliminates the cash-only barrier
- **Benefit:** Reduces booking friction; enables advance deposits; modernizes perception
- **Quick win** — 1 week

### 3. Google Business Profile + Simple Website
- **What:** Optimize Google Business listing with photos, menu, hours. Create a one-page website with room info, restaurant menu, and WhatsApp booking link
- **Benefit:** Direct discoverability; reduces 100% OTA dependency; showcases the restaurant
- **Quick win** — 1 week

---

## Manual Noise Score: 9/10

East Legon Guest Lodge is the most manually operated property in this audit. No website, no digital payments, no WhatsApp, no booking system, cash-only — every single guest interaction requires human handling via phone or OTA. The restaurant (their biggest asset) has zero digital presence. Almost every workflow is a candidate for automation.
